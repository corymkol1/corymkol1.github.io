About

I’m Cory, a business professional, developer, and entrepreneur with a diverse background in technology, accounting, customer service, hospitality, and business operations. I’ve built my career around solving problems, learning quickly, and finding practical ways to make systems, businesses, and ideas work better.

My experience includes front-end web development, bookkeeping, tax preparation, business management, technical support, and content creation. I enjoy projects that combine creativity with logic—whether I’m building a website, developing a software project, organizing financial data, improving a business process, or creating something entirely new.

As the owner of The Tax Pro FL, LLC, I’ve also gained hands-on experience managing a business, working directly with clients, and helping small businesses and self-employed professionals better understand their finances. Entrepreneurship has strengthened my skills in project management, communication, problem-solving, and strategic thinking.
